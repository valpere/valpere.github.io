## Overview

"Make it like this site" is the most common brief for a new website, and the hardest to hold to: opinions about "like" drift, and the build
slowly stops resembling the reference. This service turns the reference into something you can check: a written brief first, a measured
comparison at the end.

**Result: a new site with its own content and brand that shares the reference's structure, rhythm and feel, with the match shown in
screenshots rather than asserted.**

---

## How it works

1. **Brief of the reference.** I read the live site once, politely: page structure, section order and heights, colour roles, type scale,
   spacing, corner radii, breakpoints, which parts move and how. For a whole site I follow its main navigation to cover the distinct page
   types (home, list, detail). Result: a short document and a token file you can read and correct before anything is built.
2. **Your content and brand.** Your text, your photos, your logo, your colours where the reference's palette does not fit. The brief decides
   structure and proportions; it never supplies content.
3. **Build.** Hand-written HTML/CSS (plus a little JavaScript where behaviour needs it), as a static site, Jekyll, or a theme layer on an
   existing OpenCart store. Light and dark themes when the reference has them, Ukrainian and English versions when you need both.
4. **Measured check.** Screenshots of the reference and the build at desktop and phone widths, compared pixel by pixel on the layout
   (target: at least 98% of pixels match within tolerance and page heights within a few pixels where the structure is meant to match),
   plus a keyboard pass and a look at both colour themes. Differences are listed with the reason for each, not hidden.
5. **Hand-over.** Source repository, the deployed site, the brief and the design tokens, so the next change does not start from zero.

## What you get

- A written reference brief and a design-token file
- The built site in your repository and live on your hosting
- Responsive layout checked at desktop and phone widths, with the comparison screenshots
- Optional: bilingual UA/EN, light/dark theme, contact or lead form wired to email, Telegram or CRM

---

## Boundaries

- The reference's text, photographs, illustrations, logos and fonts are not copied; commercial fonts get a free look-alike.
- No bypassing of bot protection, and no sign-in, checkout or banking pages as references.
- A site that is far from the reference on purpose (a different audience, a different brand) is a design job, not a remake, and is quoted as one.

## Stack

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Jekyll, OpenCart, design tokens, Chromium for the visual checks
